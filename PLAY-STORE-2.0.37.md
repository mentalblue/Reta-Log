# Reta Log

Short description:
Private injection journal, peptide trends, daily routines and inventory tracking.

Full description:

Your records. Your routine. One clear view.

Reta Log brings your injection journal, plans, body trends and inventory together in a private, on-device workspace. Record what happened, see what is planned and understand your history without losing the details.

A JOURNAL THAT STAYS CONNECTED
Record date, time, compound, dose, source, injection site and notes. Planned, Taken and Skipped entries remain distinct. Update a planned injection when it is completed, with linked inventory accounting that follows edits to its dose or source.

PLANS YOU CONTROL
Create your own schedules and phases, choose dates, days and times, and review upcoming injections. Edit or delete a plan while preserving completed injection history. Reta Log does not recommend a dose or titration schedule.

EXPLORE YOUR PROGRESS
Switch between Overview, Peptides, Body and Activity. Review recorded weight and body-composition trends where data is available. Explore separate peptide estimates using an explicit half-life assumption, with planned projections clearly separate from completed injections.

INTERACTIVE CHARTS
Choose a time range, pan, zoom, select a point or open a landscape fullscreen chart. Keep controls and selected values beside the graph for a clearer view of your history.

INVENTORY WITH A TRACEABLE HISTORY
Track peptide vials, BAC water, Acetic Acid, cartridges, pens, syringes, pen needles, wipes and other items. Record the exact solvent container and volume used for reconstitution. Follow solution transfers into cartridges while retaining source links and remaining quantities. Inventory records document your entries; they do not verify content, compatibility or preparation safety.

BUILD YOUR DAILY ROUTINE
Keep protein, hydration, activity, check-ins, body measurements and notes together. Personalize supported goals, colours and home-card order. See available information on Today, then open details when you need them.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
Your records are stored on your device. Import supported CSV files, including Eufy exports. Connect compatible Health Connect data when available and permitted. Use backup, restore, export and report tools to keep a copy of your history.

CAMERA PULSE ESTIMATE
On compatible phones, estimate pulse using the rear camera and flash. Accuracy and feedback depend on hardware, lighting and finger placement. This optical estimate is not an ECG or a medical measurement device.

Reta Log is a personal tracking and educational tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent medical conditions, prescribe medication, or recommend dosing. Modeled levels are estimates, not measured blood concentrations. Consumer body-composition readings are estimates from the source device. These features do not replace professional medical advice or clinical measurements.
